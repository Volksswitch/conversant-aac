# Conversant AAC — Tester Recruitment Message

I'm building **Conversant AAC**, a free, open-source app that gives non-speaking individuals a way to hold real, back-and-forth conversation — not just answer questions or type slowly, but actually keep pace with a live conversation the way speaking people do. It works by using generative AI to instantly suggest a few natural response options based on what the other person just said; the user picks one with a tap, and the device speaks it in their voice — and it already lets the user build a personal profile of their own interests and relationships so those responses start sounding more like them. Right now I'm looking for a small group of **non-speaking individuals, age 16 or older, who can read and use touch/pointer selection**, to try it out and tell me honestly what works and what doesn't.

Here's what's involved: the app runs right in a web browser (Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome) on hardware you likely already have — a Windows tablet like a Microsoft Surface, a MacBook, or a Chromebook. No special hardware or installation required. It uses the Claude AI API, and **any cost of that API usage is covered by the project, not you** — testing costs you nothing. In return, this is a volunteer effort: the app is free and open source and always will be, so there's no product to sell and no company behind this looking to profit. What I'm asking for is your time, your honest feedback, and your patience with something that's still being actively built and improved based on exactly what testers like you tell me.

I believe this can be genuinely life-changing. Existing AAC devices leave non-speaking people locked out of real conversation — forced into slow, transactional exchanges while everyone around them talks freely. Conversant AAC is built to close that gap and give people back the experience of just... talking with someone, in real time, as themselves. If that sounds worth your time, I'd love to have you help shape it.
