# Conversant AAC — Version History

Per-release engineering detail, one row per released version. Split out of
`CLAUDE.md` on August 7 2026, where the table had grown to 20% of a file that is
loaded into context in full every session. Nothing was edited in the move — the
rows are byte-identical to what was there.

**This is the engineering record.** For the user-facing account of a release, see
`CHANGELOG.md`, which is deliberately coarser: not every patch earns a user-facing
entry, and one entry there can cover several patches here.

**Read this when you need to know why a specific version did what it did** — a
regression that bisects to a release, a decision whose reasoning is quoted by a
later note, or the history behind a subsystem. It is reference, not policy.

**The policy lives in `CLAUDE.md` → Versioning**: which digit to bump, the release
ritual, and the `bump Conversant AAC` trigger phrase. A release adds its row here
as step 4 of that ritual (see `RELEASING.md`).

**Rows are in insertion order, not version order.** The table accreted over time
and rows were sometimes added out of sequence; the order is preserved because it
records when a row was written, which version-sorting would destroy. Use your
editor's find rather than expecting a sorted list.

**Released versions only** — rows are added at bump, never during a dev cycle.
