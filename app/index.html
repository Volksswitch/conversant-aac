In Practice Mode the AI plays the other person, so you can rehearse a conversation without a microphone and without spending anyone's patience. Pick something to practice below; the conversation screen works exactly as it normally does, except Start Listening cues the other person to speak instead of opening the mic. Give them their own voice under Speech.

Edit the Express Panel shown beside this tab. Each button is a Phrase (speaks when tapped), a Partner (who you're talking with — personalizes openers and tells the AI who's there), a Feeling (sets the mood of the suggestions), or a Place (where you are).

Set the exact words spoken by the persistent control buttons, and the cards shown by Start conversation and Wind down — so each sounds like something you would say.
