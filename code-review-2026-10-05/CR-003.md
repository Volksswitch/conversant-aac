# CR-003: Answering with an Express phrase, typed words, a sound or a repair card leaves the partner's words in the listening buffer, so the next Listen tap re-records them

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** high
- **Category:** bug
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed; reproducer: reproduced)
- **Location:** `app/js/app.js:1689`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/app.js:3712 speakAsUserTurn stt.stopListening() without resetTranscript`; `app/js/app.js:5282 playAudioTurn stt.stopListening() without resetTranscript`; `app/js/app.js:2182 handleRepairOfSelf stt.stopListening() without resetTranscript`; `app/js/app.js:2064-2071 handleResponseSelected (does reset — the correct pattern)`; `app/js/stt.js:724 stopListening keeps accumulatedText`; `app/js/app.js:340 heardPartnerText`; `app/js/app.js:3712 (merged duplicate: Speaking an Express phrase or composed sentence mid-conversation leaves the partner's words in the listening buffer, so the next tap records them again)`; `app/js/app.js:3714 (merged duplicate: After answering with an Express phrase or typed words, the next Listen carries the partner's previous words into the new turn)`
- **Source:** app-b-02, stt-04, live-runtime-03

**What a user notices.** With the default setting (listening does not resume by itself), if the user answers with an Express button, their own typed words, a sound, or a repeat card, the next time they tap Listen the other person's previous sentence is glued onto the front of whatever they say next. It shows up twice in the conversation, is saved twice, and the AI answers the old question again.

**What is wrong.** toggleListening decides 'mid-turn pause' vs 'new turn' by whether heardPartnerText() is non-empty, which relies on every floor change clearing the STT buffer (CLAUDE.md: 'a partner turn ends at a FLOOR CHANGE ... Each of those ... calls stt.resetTranscript() explicitly'). handleResponseSelected does reset (lines 2064-2071, with a comment explaining stopListening keeps accumulatedText). But speakAsUserTurn (Express phrase + composer Speak), playAudioTurn (sound buttons) and handleRepairOfSelf only call stt.stopListening(). When auto-resume is off (the default), they end in resumeOrIdle() -> idle, so startFreshListening (which resets) never runs. The buffer still holds the committed partner turn. The next Listen tap takes the resumePartnerCapture branch, stt.resumeListening() keeps the buffer, and the next checkpoint hands 'old turn + new speech' to handleSilencePeriod. Because commitExchange already detached the old pending entry, logPartnerInterim creates a NEW partner entry containing the old words again. Reproduced with app/js/stt.js and the test fake recognizer: after stopListening the buffer was 'How was your weekend?' and after resumeListening + new speech the checkpoint text was 'How was your weekend? That sounds great.'

**How it happens.** Auto-resume off (default). Tap Listen; partner says "How was your weekend?"; cards appear. Tap the Express phrase "Good, thanks." It is spoken and committed (partner turn + user turn). Mic is now off. Partner says "That's great." User taps Listen -> resumePartnerCapture. Checkpoint text = "How was your weekend? That's great." The pane shows the partner's question again inside the new turn, the saved file gets a second partner entry containing it, and the AI generates replies to the old question.

> **Verifier correction to the trigger:** For sound buttons the duplication only happens when the microphone was already off (paused mid-turn) when the sound was tapped; with the mic on, playAudioTurn's unmute path calls startFreshListening, which resets the buffer. The Express phrase and composer Speak paths behave as described.

**Suggested fix.** In each floor-changing path, mirror handleResponseSelected: right after capturing `raw` and calling stt.stopListening(), call stt.resetTranscript() (skip it only when practiceMode, as handleResponseSelected does). Concretely: speakAsUserTurn after line 3712; playAudioTurn after line 5282 (but only on the success path — on `!result.played` it resumes the SAME turn with resumePartnerCapture, so reset must happen after the `if (!result.played)` early return, i.e. move the reset to just after `if (result.aborted) return;`); handleRepairOfSelf after line 2182 (also capture `raw` with heardPartnerText() before the stop rather than currentPartnerText at 2228). Do NOT add a reset to the defers path of handleResponseSelected (lines 2016-2025) — that one intentionally keeps the turn open.

**How to verify the fix.** Add a test to tests/stt.test.mjs documenting the contract: startListening, emitFinal('A'), stopListening, assert getCurrentTranscript()==='A' (buffer survives stop) and after resetTranscript() it is ''. Then add a source-level guard in tests/placeholders.test.mjs asserting that speakAsUserTurn, playAudioTurn and handleRepairOfSelf bodies (comments stripped) contain `stt.resetTranscript()`. Manual: run the failure scenario above with auto-resume off and confirm the second partner turn contains only the new words in both the pane and the saved JSON.

**Do not break.** The Listen stop/start PAUSE behavior (CLAUDE.md 'Listen stop/start is a PAUSE') must keep working: only reset on floor changes, never in toggleListening's stop branch. A failed sound clip must still resume the same turn. Practice Mode has no mic; follow handleResponseSelected's `if (!practiceMode)` guard.

<details><summary>Merged duplicate stt-04: Speaking an Express phrase or composed sentence mid-conversation leaves the partner's words in the listening buffer, so the next tap records them again</summary>

stt.stopListening() deliberately keeps accumulatedText (pause semantics), and app.js heardPartnerText() reads `stt.getCurrentTranscript()` first. handleResponseSelected() therefore calls stt.resetTranscript() right after stopping (comment at 2066-2070 records the July 10 2026 bug this prevents). speakAsUserTurn() (Express phrases and 'In my own words' Speak) and playAudioTurn() (sound buttons) capture `raw = heardPartnerText()`, call stt.stopListening(), commit, and - when the turn does not open the conversation and auto-resume is off (the default, loadAutoRelisten() ?? false) - end in resumeOrIdle()'s idle branch without ever resetting the STT buffer. The next Express tap reads the same partner text again as `raw` and placePartnerTurn() appends it as a new partner turn (pendingPartnerHistoryIdx was reset to -1). The next Listen tap sees heardPartnerText() non-empty and takes resumePartnerCapture(), which keeps the stale words at the front of the next partner turn.

Fix as proposed by that reviewer: In speakAsUserTurn(), immediately after `stt.stopListening();` add `stt.resetTranscript();` (mirroring handleResponseSelected), guarded by `if (!practiceMode)` like that function. In playAudioTurn(), add `stt.resetTranscript();` only AFTER the `if (!result.played) {...return;}` check (the not-played branch resumes capture and must keep the buffer), e.g. right after `if (result.aborted) return;`.

</details>

<details><summary>Merged duplicate live-runtime-03: After answering with an Express phrase or typed words, the next Listen carries the partner's previous words into the new turn</summary>

speakAsUserTurn reads the partner's speech and calls stt.stopListening(), but unlike handleResponseSelected it never calls stt.resetTranscript(). stopListening deliberately keeps accumulatedText. With auto-resume off, resumeOrIdle leaves the mic idle. When the user then taps Listen, toggleListening tests `heardPartnerText()` (which prefers stt.getCurrentTranscript()) - the stale buffer is non-empty, so it takes the 'mid-turn pause' branch resumePartnerCapture() instead of startFreshListening(), and the new partner speech is appended to the previous turn's text. The stt.js comment states the invariant 'every floor change ... calls resetTranscript() explicitly', which this path violates.

Fix as proposed by that reviewer: In speakAsUserTurn, immediately after `stt.stopListening();` add `stt.resetTranscript();` (same as handleResponseSelected, guarded the same way: only when not in practice mode, i.e. wrap as `if (!practiceMode) { stt.stopListening(); stt.resetTranscript(); }` or keep stopListening as is and add the reset). Apply the same to playAudioTurn after `stt.stopListening();   // the mute` - but only on the path where the sound actually played and the turn is committed (after the `if (!result.played)` early return, which must keep the buffer because it resumes capture).

</details>
