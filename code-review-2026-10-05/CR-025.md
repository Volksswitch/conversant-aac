# CR-025: Starting a practice scenario keeps the real conversation's Partner, Feeling and goals switched on

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** medium
- **Category:** bug
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/js/app.js:2555`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/app.js:2559-2566`; `app/js/app.js:2759-2830`; `app/js/app.js:3646`; `app/js/app.js:3672-3684`; `app/js/app.js:4352-4390`; `app/js/app.js:3672 (merged duplicate: Goals and the feeling survive into a new conversation started with Start conversation)`
- **Source:** app-c-02, app-d-08

**What a user notices.** If a partner such as Mom (or a feeling or goal) was lit when the user starts a practice role-play like ordering coffee, the practice suggestions are still written as if the user were talking to Mom, with Mom's goals and personal openers, which makes the rehearsal unrealistic and confusing.

**What is wrong.** The comment in startPractice says the partner is set 'AFTER the teardown, which clears the partner', but terminateConversation() never clears activePartner, activeFeeling or activeGoals - only clearInfluencers() does, and that is called from handleEndConversation, not from startPractice. For a scenario with no personId, the real conversation's activePartner survives into practice; buildSituationBlock then tells the model 'You are currently talking with <real partner>' plus that person's buildPartnerBlock profile and any switched-on goals, alongside the practice role-play instruction. applyControlPhrases() (called in startPractice) also merges the stale partner's personal openers/closings into the practice openers. For a scenario WITH a personId, activePartner is replaced by direct assignment, bypassing whatever normally drops partner-sourced goals, and activeFeeling still persists.

**How it happens.** 1) On the conversation screen tap the Express Panel partner button 'Mom' and a feeling 'Tired'. 2) Without pressing End conversation, open Settings > Practice and start a built-in scenario such as a coffee shop. 3) Tap Start Listening: the Express Panel still shows Mom and Tired lit, and the suggested responses address Mom / reflect Mom's per-partner profile in a barista role-play.

**Suggested fix.** In startPractice, right after `await terminateConversation();`, call `clearInfluencers();` (it clears activePartner, activeFeeling, activeGoals, re-renders the panel and re-applies control phrases), then set the scenario's person as today. Fix the comment to say the influencers are cleared here explicitly. Leave activePlace alone (place deliberately persists, per CLAUDE.md).

**How to verify the fix.** Manual: steps above; after the fix the panel shows no partner/feeling lit when practice starts (unless the scenario has a person, in which case only that person is the partner) and a captured request's system text contains no 'You are currently talking with Mom'. Optionally a source-level guard in tests/placeholders.test.mjs asserting startPractice calls clearInfluencers().

**Do not break.** Place must NOT be cleared (CLAUDE.md My Places: place persists across conversations). clearInfluencers re-runs applyControlPhrases, and startPractice resets practiceOpeners afterwards - keep `practiceOpeners = []; applyControlPhrases();` after the person is set so the scenario person's phrases are merged.

**Design reference.** CLAUDE.md, My Places ('Partner and Feeling are properties OF the conversation'); Per-partner profile; Practice Mode - full plan

**Verification notes.** terminateConversation never touches activePartner/activeFeeling/activeGoals; only clearInfluencers() does, and it is called from handleEndConversation, not startPractice. buildSituationBlock adds 'You are currently talking with <label>' plus buildPartnerBlock, goals and feeling whenever they are set, and practice uses the same generation path. applyControlPhrases merges the active partner's openers. The comment 'Set AFTER the teardown, which clears the partner' is wrong. Settings (and therefore Practice) is reachable with a partner lit.

<details><summary>Merged duplicate app-d-08: Goals and the feeling survive into a new conversation started with Start conversation</summary>

clearInfluencers() is the only place activeGoals is cleared and activeFeeling nulled, and it is called only from End conversation. The Start-conversation path that closes a live conversation (wasOpener && pendingNewConversation) calls terminateConversation() only, deliberately keeping the partner for personalized openers - but goals and feeling are also kept. The design record says every goal goes when its conversation ends ('A conversation goal is a property OF the conversation by definition, so it cannot outlive it') and feeling, like partner, is a property of the conversation (v0.5.31). General goals in particular survive partner and place changes by design, so nothing else removes them. Same for starting a Practice scenario.

Fix as proposed by that reviewer: In the wasOpener && pendingNewConversation branch, after `await terminateConversation();` add `activeGoals.clear(); activeFeeling = null; renderExpressPanel();` (leave activePartner alone - it personalizes the opener, per the comment at 3643). In startPractice, after terminateConversation(), also `activeGoals.clear();` before the practice partner is set.


Verifier correction: Beyond the suggested fix: in startPractice, a scenario with no personId also keeps whatever REAL partner was active before. buildSituationBlock then shapes the practice suggestions with that person's profile and goals. Clear activePartner, activeFeeling and activeGoals after terminateConversation() in startPractice, then set the practice partner if there is one.
