# CR-064: Speaking, Listen and other actions during a playing sound do not stop it, so the mute is broken

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** medium
- **Category:** design-violation
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/js/app.js:5225`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/app.js:3711-3744 (speakAsUserTurn: no stopExpressAudio, ends in startFreshListening)`; `app/js/app.js:1665-1696 (toggleListening start branch)`; `app/js/app.js:266-269 (speakUserStatement finally clears speakingUserStatement)`; `app/js/app.js:1966 (handleResponseSelected)`; `app/js/app.js:2760 (only caller of stopExpressAudio besides the sound button)`; `app/js/app.js:3711 (merged duplicate: Tapping a reply card, Express phrase or composer Speak while a sound clip plays does not stop the clip, and reopens the microphone while it is still playing)`
- **Source:** app-e-02, x-async-10

**What a user notices.** If the user taps a phrase, a response card, 'In my own words' Speak, or Listen while a recorded sound is still playing, the sound keeps playing under or over the app's voice and the microphone can turn back on while the recording is still audible, so the app may treat the recording as the other person talking. The other person's last line can also be saved twice.

**What is wrong.** stopExpressAudio is called only from handlePlayAudioItem and terminateConversation. No other floor-taking path stops a playing clip: speakAsUserTurn (Express phrases, composer Speak), handleResponseSelected (cards), the choice chips, and toggleListening all ignore audioPlayer. Consequences: (1) TTS speaks over the clip; (2) speakUserStatement's finally sets speakingUserStatement=false, removing the placeholder gate that playClip set for the clip; (3) speakAsUserTurn ends in startFreshListening()/resumeOrIdle(), and toggleListening's start branch opens the mic - while the clip is still audible, defeating the documented mute (the app ignores its own speech by matching words, which it cannot do for a recording); (4) playAudioTurn captured raw=heardPartnerText() at the tap and commits it when the clip ends, but the phrase path already committed the same partner text, so the partner turn is duplicated and the clip's turn lands after the phrase that was tapped later.

**How it happens.** Auto-resume on. Partner says 'What should we listen to?'. User taps a 2-minute music sound button (mic mutes). Ten seconds in, user taps the Express phrase 'This one!'. The phrase is spoken over the music, committed with the partner's line, then startFreshListening() reopens the microphone; the still-playing music is transcribed as partner speech and the AI offers replies to it. When the music ends, playAudioTurn commits 'What should we listen to?' again plus the sound turn, after 'This one!'.

**Suggested fix.** Add a single helper used by every path that takes the floor or opens the mic: before speaking or listening, call `stopExpressAudio()` if `audioPlayer` is set. Concretely: at the top of speakAsUserTurn (before `const raw = heardPartnerText()`), in handleResponseSelected before it speaks, in the choice-chip / reframe / regenerate handlers that call speakAsUserTurn indirectly, and in toggleListening's start branch. Because stopping a clip resolves playAudioTurn asynchronously, also make playAudioTurn skip its own listening restore and its commit of `raw` when the clip was stopped by another action (e.g. set `audioPlayer.preempted = true` in the helper; in playAudioTurn, if preempted, still commit the sound turn with raw='' (the other path commits the partner text) and return without touching listening).

> **Verifier correction to the fix (takes precedence):** Route the helper through stopExpressAudio with a 'preempted' flag, as suggested. Also make playAudioTurn skip its own startFreshListening/captureAfterUserSpeaks block when preempted, so two paths do not both restart listening. The preempted sound turn should still be committed BEFORE the phrase turn: the phrase path awaits speech, so the clip's commit runs first only if the clip's continuation is awaited or committed synchronously. Consider committing the sound turn inside the helper rather than relying on async ordering.

**How to verify the fix.** Manual (app.js is not unit-testable): with Listen on and auto-resume on, start a long sound, then tap a phrase; the sound must stop before the phrase is spoken, the sound turn must appear before the phrase in the pane and saved file, the partner line must appear once, and the microphone must not reopen until after the phrase. Repeat with a response card and with the Listen button. Add a source-level guard in tests/express-audio.test.mjs that speakAsUserTurn's body calls stopExpressAudio.

**Do not break.** Do not cancel TTS that the sound path itself depends on; keep the abort path in terminateConversation dropping the turn; nothing may be awaited before el.play() in the sound tap path.

**Design reference.** CLAUDE.md 'SOUND buttons on the Express Panel': 'A tap MUTES THE MICROPHONE ... A REAL MUTE, NOT "THE APP IS TALKING"'; 'Holding phrases stay quiet during playback'.

**Verification notes.** A grep finds stopExpressAudio called only at app.js:2760 (terminateConversation) and 5236-5237 (handlePlayAudioItem). No other reference to audioPlayer exists apart from the panel's playingAudioId, and ui.js only uses that to show the button as pressed. speakAsUserTurn (3691-3746) never checks audioPlayer. Its speakUserStatement finally block clears speakingUserStatement, and it ends in startFreshListening or resumeOrIdle, which can open the mic while the clip is still audible. toggleListening's start branch has no audio check either. Both speakAsUserTurn and playAudioTurn capture heardPartnerText() and commit it. stopListening does not clear the transcript, and the first commit resets pendingPartnerHistoryIdx, so the partner line is appended twice, and the sound turn lands after the later phrase.

<details><summary>Merged duplicate x-async-10: Tapping a reply card, Express phrase or composer Speak while a sound clip plays does not stop the clip, and reopens the microphone while it is still playing</summary>

Only terminateConversation and another sound tap stop a playing clip. handleResponseSelected and speakAsUserTurn speak over it, then call resumeOrIdle/startFreshListening (auto-resume on) which reopens the microphone while the clip continues - defeating the 'real mute' decision, since the echo filter has no words for a recording. When the clip later ends, playClip sets speakingUserStatement = false even if a spoken statement is in progress, and playAudioTurn commits the clip's turn after the spoken turn and may call startFreshListening again (resetting the new partner turn).

Fix as proposed by that reviewer: At the top of speakAsUserTurn, handleResponseSelected (after the response.op branch) and openComposer, call `stopExpressAudio();` so taking the floor with words ends the clip (its turn is then committed as 'stopped' by its own playAudioTurn before the new turn). In playAudioTurn, skip the listening restoration (`if (wasListening) startFreshListening()` and the captureAfterUserSpeaks branch) when another statement is in progress (`if (speakingUserStatement) return;` after commitExchange).
