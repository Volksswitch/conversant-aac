# CR-070: Catchphrase redaction at the voice-harvest boundary was never built

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** medium
- **Category:** design-violation
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/js/voice-harvest.js:76`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/voice-refresh.js:24-33 (Express list used only to classify legacy turns)`; `app/js/voice.js:351-357 (harvested exemplars sent with 'Follow their phrasing')`; `app/js/express-panel.js:230-240 (userAuthoredItems claims to feed 'the catchphrase redaction list')`; `Documents/Conversant AAC Sounds Like Me.docx §5.1 consequence 2 and the row 'Also the catchphrase redaction list'`
- **Source:** graph-voice-04

**What a user notices.** Once the app learns from what the user types, their personal catchphrases (the ones they keep on Express Panel buttons so they choose when to say them) can be copied by the AI into suggested replies, making the device sound like it is using the user's signature lines on its own.

**What is wrong.** The design (CLAUDE.md 'CATCHPHRASES ARE EXPRESS PANEL BUTTONS, AND THE AI NEVER PRODUCES THEM' and Sounds Like Me §5.1) says Phase 2 harvests composed prose, that prose contains the user's catchphrases, so the prohibition 'needs enforcing at the harvest boundary' by stripping the user's own (provenance-filtered) Express phrases from harvested samples before they reach the model. collectExemplars takes composed and reworded sentences verbatim; the only use of the Express list in the harvest is exact-match classification of legacy turns without a source (classifyTurn). express-panel.userAuthoredItems documents itself as feeding 'the catchphrase redaction list' but nothing consumes it for that. voice.buildBlock then sends the exemplars with 'Follow their phrasing, rhythm and level of detail', which invites exactly the over-application the decision forbids. Today only Conversation Review 'typed'/reworded answers reach this path (because of the source bug), but they can already carry catchphrases, and fixing the source bug makes every composed sentence flow through.

**Evidence.**

```
app/js/voice-harvest.js:84-89 `const text = String(turn.selectedText || '').trim(); if (words(text).length < MIN_EXEMPLAR_WORDS) continue; ... out.push(text);`
app/js/voice-refresh.js:31-32 `expressPhrases: expressPanel.allItems().filter((i) => i.type === 'phrase' && i.text).map((i) => i.text),` (used only by classifyTurn's legacy fallback, voice-harvest.js:68)
app/js/voice.js:356 `'Follow their phrasing, rhythm and level of detail. ...'`
app/js/express-panel.js:232-233 `Feeds the voice block (Sounds Like Me Phase 0), the catchphrase redaction list, ...`
```

**How it happens.** User has a self-made Express button "Let's go!". In Conversation Review they type the better answer "Let's go! I'll meet you at the game Saturday." and leave the review. The harvested exemplar list (and the system prompt) contains the sentence with "Let's go!" intact, under 'Follow their phrasing'.

**Suggested fix.** Do the redaction where the list is always current: voice.buildBlock(idiom) already receives the user-authored Express phrases. Before listing harvested exemplars, map each through a helper `redactCatchphrases(text, idiom)` that removes every case-insensitive whole-phrase occurrence of each idiom string (escape regex characters; allow trailing punctuation; collapse leftover whitespace and stray leading/trailing punctuation), then drop any exemplar that falls below 4 words (reuse MIN_EXEMPLAR_WORDS - export it from voice-harvest.js or duplicate the constant with a comment). Put the helper in voice-harvest.js as a pure export so it is unit-testable. Use only the provenance-filtered user-authored list (never shipped defaults such as 'Yes' or 'Thank you').

> **Verifier correction to the fix (takes precedence):** The approach is sound. Do the redaction in buildBlock against the `idiom` argument (app.js:4327 already builds it from expressPanel.userAuthoredItems()), not allItems. Note buildBlock also caps idiom to 20 for display (idiom.slice(0, 20)); redact against the full idiom list, not the sliced one.

**How to verify the fix.** tests/voice-harvest.test.mjs: redactCatchphrases("Let's go! I'll meet you Saturday.", ["Let's go!"]) === "I'll meet you Saturday."; a default phrase not in the list is untouched. tests for voice.buildBlock: with a harvest exemplar containing an idiom phrase, the output exemplar lacks it; one that drops below 4 words is omitted.

**Do not break.** Redact against userAuthoredItems only, never allItems (that includes shipped defaults and would strip 'Yes', 'Thank you'). Do not alter the stored harvest or the dismissed list; redaction is output-time only. Keep the idiom list itself in the block (deliberate Phase 0 decision).

**Design reference.** CLAUDE.md 'CATCHPHRASES ARE EXPRESS PANEL BUTTONS, AND THE AI NEVER PRODUCES THEM' and 'PROVENANCE MARKING is therefore a dependency of Phase 0'; Sounds Like Me.docx §5.1.

**Verification notes.** No redaction exists anywhere: grep for 'redact'/'catchphrase' in voice-harvest.js, voice.js, voice-refresh.js finds only the comment in express-panel.js:232 claiming userAuthoredItems feeds 'the catchphrase redaction list', and nothing consumes it for that. collectExemplars pushes composed/reworded text verbatim; voice-refresh uses the Express list only for legacy classification; buildBlock (voice.js:351-357) emits harvested exemplars under 'Follow their phrasing'. The Sounds Like Me document (paragraphs 195 and 322) and CLAUDE.md both say redaction at the harvest boundary is required. Not in TODO.md. Reachable today through Conversation Review typed/reworded answers (reviewedTurns), and for every composed sentence once graph-voice-01 is fixed. Impact is partly mitigated by the idiom block's 'Do NOT put these exact phrases into responses' and the exemplar block's 'do not repeat their content', which is why the design wanted enforcement rather than a prompt plea; medium is reasonable.
