# CR-078: Ending the conversation while a chosen card is still being spoken puts that turn into the conversation after it has ended

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** medium
- **Category:** race-condition
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/js/app.js:2077`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/app.js:2084 commitExchange after speech`; `app/js/app.js:2104-2120 opener/closing branches`; `app/js/app.js:2759-2830 terminateConversation`; `app/js/app.js:5269 playClip aborted flag (the precedent)`; `app/js/app.js:3718-3726 speakAsUserTurn has the same shape`; `app/js/app.js:2233-2252 handleRepairOfSelf same shape`; `app/js/app.js:2077 (merged duplicate: Pressing End conversation while the user's statement is still being spoken does not end it: the turn is saved into a new conversation and the microphone turns on)`; `app/js/app.js:2077 (merged duplicate: Ending the conversation while a reply is being spoken leaves that exchange in the new, supposedly empty conversation and starts a new saved file with it)`
- **Source:** app-b-04, live-runtime-04, x-async-05

**What a user notices.** If the user taps a goodbye card and then taps End conversation while it is still being said (a natural thing to do), the goodbye is cut off, then reappears on the freshly cleared screen with the other person's last words, the goodbye cards come back, and the saved record gets the other person's line twice. If it was an opening line, the microphone switches itself on after End.

**What is wrong.** handleResponseSelected awaits speakUserStatement(response.text) and then unconditionally runs engine.selectResponse, commitExchange and the follow-up branch. terminateConversation (End) calls tts.cancel() which resolves the speak promise, and synchronously clears conversationHistory, the engine, the palette, sets manualListenArmed=false and detaches/finalizes the pending partner entry. When handleResponseSelected resumes it has no way to know the conversation ended: commitExchange pushes the old `raw` partner text and the user turn into the now-empty history (rendered on screen), detachPendingPartnerTurn returns null so finalizePartnerTurn(null) APPENDS a second partner entry (into the old log if its id has not been reset yet, otherwise lazily starting a new conversation file), then logUserResponse. The wasClosing branch calls offerClosings() so goodbye cards reappear after End; the wasOpener branch sets manualListenArmed=true and calls startFreshListening(), opening the mic. Express sound already handles this with `audioPlayer.aborted` (CLAUDE.md: 'ending a conversation under a playing clip drops the turn'), but spoken cards do not.

**How it happens.** Partner: "Well, I should get going." Goodbye cards appear. User taps "Bye! Take care." and, while it is speaking, taps End conversation. Speech is cut off; status says the conversation ended; then the pane shows the partner's line and "Bye! Take care." again on the empty screen, the goodbye cards return, and the saved conversation has the partner line twice (once finalized by End, once appended by commitExchange). Same with an opener: the mic turns on after End.

**Suggested fix.** Add a conversation epoch: `let conversationEpoch = 0;` incremented at the top of terminateConversation(). In handleResponseSelected capture `const epoch = conversationEpoch;` AFTER the `await terminateConversation()` in the opener-with-pendingNewConversation branch (that one is the intended ending) and before `await speakUserStatement(response.text)`; immediately after the await, `if (epoch !== conversationEpoch) return;` (drop the turn, like the aborted sound). Apply the same check after the speech await in speakAsUserTurn (line 3718) and handleRepairOfSelf (line 2233), and in the defers path after line 2020.

**How to verify the fix.** Manual: in Practice Mode or a live session, tap a goodbye card and immediately tap End conversation; confirm the pane stays empty, no goodbye cards reappear, the Listen button stays off, and the previous conversation file has exactly one copy of the partner line. Add a source-level guard in tests/placeholders.test.mjs that handleResponseSelected and speakAsUserTurn compare an epoch after their speech await.

**Do not break.** The opener path deliberately calls terminateConversation itself before speaking (the deferred ending); the epoch must be captured after that call or every opener over a live conversation would be dropped. Do not break the 'Start conversation is a toggle' behavior.

**Design reference.** CLAUDE.md 'SOUND buttons on the Express Panel' (ending a conversation under a playing clip drops the turn); 'Transcript mirrors the conversation pane'.

**Verification notes.** handleEndConversation/terminateConversation has no guard for speakingUserStatement (only whileComposerClosed). terminateConversation calls tts.cancel(), which resolves the pending speak (builtin onend/onerror -> finish -> resolve; paid path catch returns when the token moved). It clears history, engine, palette, sets manualListenArmed=false, and detaches+finalizes the pending partner entry (pendingRaw is '' because handleResponseSelected already reset the buffer, but the pending handle's rawTranscript is used). handleResponseSelected then resumes after `await speakUserStatement`: commitExchange pushes raw + user text into the cleared history and renders it, detachPendingPartnerTurn now returns null so finalizePartnerTurn(null) appends a second partner entry (lazily starting a new log if the id was reset), and the wasClosing branch calls offerClosings(), wasOpener sets manualListenArmed=true and startFreshListening(). playClip's aborted flag is the existing precedent; spoken cards lack it.

<details><summary>Merged duplicate live-runtime-04: Pressing End conversation while the user's statement is still being spoken does not end it: the turn is saved into a new conversation and the microphone turns on</summary>

Every 'speak as the user' handler awaits speakUserStatement() and then commits the turn and resumes listening without checking whether the conversation ended meanwhile. End conversation calls tts.cancel() (which resolves the pending speak via onerror) and terminateConversation(), which clears history and resets the conversation id. The suspended handler then resumes: engine.selectResponse, commitExchange (which lazily starts a NEW conversation log file containing the turn), and for an opener `manualListenArmed = true; startFreshListening();` - reopening the microphone. Express sounds already guard this case (playAudioTurn returns when result.aborted), but spoken statements do not.

Fix as proposed by that reviewer: Add a module-level `let conversationEpoch = 0;` incremented at the top of terminateConversation(). In each handler capture `const epoch = conversationEpoch;` before the `await speakUserStatement(...)` and, right after it, `if (epoch !== conversationEpoch) return;` - in handleResponseSelected (before engine.selectResponse at 2082; note the opener path calls terminateConversation itself BEFORE speaking, so capture the epoch after that block at line 2055), speakAsUserTurn (after line 3722), handleSayAgain (after 3227), handlePardon (after 3298), handleRepairOfSelf (after 2200 and 2233), and the defers branch (after 2020).


Verifier correction: The epoch approach is right. Also guard the commit/resume in each handler and not only the engine call, because the pane, history and log writes all follow the await. In handleRepairOfSelf add the check after the llm.repairSelf await too (the rephrase round trip can outlive an End). In handleResponseSelected capture the epoch after the deferred terminateConversation() block (the opener path), as the reviewer notes.

<details><summary>Merged duplicate x-async-05: Ending the conversation while a reply is being spoken leaves that exchange in the new, supposedly empty conversation and starts a new saved file with it</summary>

handleResponseSelected (and speakAsUserTurn, handleSayAgain, handlePardon, handleRepairOfSelf) capture `raw` before speaking, await speakUserStatement, then unconditionally call engine.selectResponse and commitExchange. If End conversation runs during the speech, terminateConversation cancels TTS (which resolves the awaited promise), empties conversationHistory, resets the engine and calls storage.resetConversationId(). The continuation then pushes the old partner turn and the user turn into the emptied history and renders them, and storage.logUserResponse lazily starts a NEW conversation file containing them (finalizePartnerTurn with the detached handle may also append). Nothing checks whether the conversation was ended during the await. The sound-button path already handles exactly this with its `aborted` flag; the speech paths do not.

Fix as proposed by that reviewer: Add `let conversationEpoch = 0;` and increment it at the top of terminateConversation(). In each speaking handler capture `const epoch = conversationEpoch;` immediately before `await speakUserStatement(...)` (in handleResponseSelected capture it AFTER the `if (wasOpener && pendingNewConversation) { await terminateConversation(); ... }` block), and right after the await add `if (epoch !== conversationEpoch) return;`. Apply to handleResponseSelected (both the defers branch and the main branch), speakAsUserTurn, handleSayAgain, handlePardon and handleRepairOfSelf.

</details>
