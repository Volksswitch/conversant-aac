# CR-088: Double-tap "armed" look is invisible or broken on many Express Panel buttons (cascade order)

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** medium
- **Category:** ui-inconsistency
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/css/styles.css:2514`
- **Also affects:** `app/css/styles.css:2445 (.ep-btn.ep-goal.ep-on fill wins over armed)`; `app/css/styles.css:2520 (.ep-btn.ep-imow fill declared after armed)`; `app/css/styles.css:2533 (.ep-btn.ep-choice fill declared after armed)`; `app/css/styles.css:2559 (.ep-btn.ep-on box-shadow declared after armed, replaces the armed ring)`; `app/css/styles.css:2578-2588 (.ep-btn.ep-band-context.ep-partner/.ep-place/.ep-feeling and .ep-band-context.ep-on are 0,3,0)`; `app/css/styles.css:5161 (.ep-btn:hover inside @media (hover: hover) is later, same specificity)`; `app/js/app.js:858-862 (tapGuard rule arms #epGrid .ep-btn with class ep-armed)`
- **Source:** css-01

**What a user notices.** With "One tap or two" set to two taps, tapping a lit partner, place, feeling, goal or offered-choice button once shows no change at all, so the person thinks the tap missed and may never manage to switch it off. On a Surface with its keyboard cover attached, a phrase button tapped once can go completely blank (white words on a white button).

**What is wrong.** tap-guard.js arms an Express Panel button by adding the class ep-armed (app.js line 859). The only rule for it is `.ep-btn.ep-armed` (specificity 0,2,0) at line 2514. Every state rule that should lose to it either has higher specificity or is declared later with equal specificity, so it wins instead: (1) Context-band buttons: `.ep-btn.ep-band-context.ep-partner/.ep-place/.ep-feeling` (0,3,0) resets background and color, leaving only a 3px ring at 35% opacity. (2) Any ON button: `.ep-btn.ep-on` (0,2,0, line 2559, later) replaces the armed box-shadow, and the ON fill rules (`.ep-btn.ep-band-context.ep-on`, `.ep-btn.ep-goal.ep-on`, both 0,3,0) replace the armed fill - the armed and un-armed ON button are pixel-identical. (3) Choice chips (`.ep-btn.ep-choice`, line 2533) and the compose cell (`.ep-btn.ep-imow`, line 2520) are declared after the armed rule and repaint the fill, so only the faint ring remains (and none at all once a choice is chosen, because .ep-on replaces the ring). (4) Inside `@media (hover: hover)`, `.ep-btn:hover` (line 5161, 0,2,0, later) sets background to --surface-raised but not color, so an armed phrase under the pointer keeps the armed white text on a white fill. A Surface with its cover attached matches (hover: hover), and Chromium leaves :hover stuck on the tapped element after a finger tap, so this is the primary device. Measured in headless Chrome against the real stylesheet: an ON context partner computed identical background/box-shadow with and without ep-armed; an armed phrase under the pointer computed background rgb(255,255,255) and color rgb(255,255,255).

**Evidence.**


**How it happens.** 1) Settings > General > One tap or two: two taps. 2) Start; tap a partner button in the Context band so it lights (needs two taps). 3) Tap the lit partner button once to switch it off: nothing visibly changes; the person waits, taps again after the double-tap interval, which only re-arms it. Same with a lit goal, a chosen choice chip. 4) On a Surface with the type cover on (or any mouse), tap a phrase once: the button turns white with white text while the pointer stays over it.

**Suggested fix.** Delete the rule at line 2514 and add a replacement AFTER the `.ep-btn.ep-on.ep-picked` block (line 2632), id-qualified so it outranks every class-only state rule including the hover rules in the @media (hover: hover) block (1,2,0 beats 0,3,0):

#epGrid .ep-btn.ep-armed {
    background: var(--ep-color);
    color: var(--ink-inverse);
    outline: 4px solid var(--info);
    outline-offset: -4px;
}
/* A button that is already filled (switched on, a chosen or offered choice, the compose
   cell) inverts to the plain surface when armed, so arming always changes something. */
#epGrid .ep-btn.ep-armed.ep-on,
#epGrid .ep-btn.ep-armed.ep-choice,
#epGrid .ep-btn.ep-armed.ep-imow {
    background: var(--surface-raised);
    color: var(--ink);
}

Use outline drawn inside the box (offset -4px) rather than an outer box-shadow, so it is neither replaced by the .ep-on ring nor covered by neighbouring cells at the default gap of 0. Keep the comment explaining it is paint only.

> **Verifier correction to the fix (takes precedence):** The id-qualified rule works (1,2,0 beats every class-only state and hover rule). One refinement: an un-lit Context partner/place/feeling armed under the first block turns a solid --ep-color fill with white text, which is close to how the band draws a LIT button; keep the --info inner outline in the fix so armed is never mistaken for ON, and check this case by eye.

**How to verify the fix.** Add a case to tests/styles.test.mjs that parses styles.css and asserts (a) the ep-armed selector is id-qualified with #epGrid and (b) its position in the file is after `.ep-btn.ep-band-context.ep-on` and `.ep-btn.ep-on`. Manually, in the browser at 1280x800: set two-tap mode, light a Context partner, tap it once and confirm it visibly changes (white fill with blue inner outline); repeat for a goal, a choice chip, a phrase and the compose cell; with a mouse resting on an armed phrase confirm the label stays readable. Compare getBoundingClientRect of every #epGrid cell armed vs not armed - must be identical.

**Do not break.** Paint only: no padding, border width or margin may change (Rule 1/23, keyguard holes). The ON state (Rule 6) must still look different from the armed state. Do not apply in Settings (tap-guard ignores taps inside #settingsDialog anyway). Do not reuse the --info outer ring of .tap-armed as-is: at the default gap of 0 it is covered by neighbouring cells.

**Design reference.** CLAUDE.md UI Layout Rule 10 (One tap or two, app-wide, Oct 1 2026) and Rule 6 (latched action shown as selected); tap-guard.js header

**Verification notes.** tap-guard.js arms #epGrid .ep-btn with class ep-armed (app.js:858-862) only in two-tap mode, and lit toggles are not exempt (only undefined cells and a playing sound are). The sole armed rule is .ep-btn.ep-armed (0,2,0) at styles.css:2514. Later equal-specificity rules .ep-btn.ep-imow (2520), .ep-btn.ep-choice (2533), .ep-btn.ep-partner/.ep-feeling (2548) and .ep-btn.ep-on (2559, box-shadow) all override it, and .ep-btn.ep-band-context.ep-partner/.ep-place/.ep-feeling and .ep-band-context.ep-on / .ep-goal.ep-on are 0,3,0. So an ON Context or goal button gets background from the 0,3,0 ON rule and box-shadow from .ep-on whether armed or not: no visible change. Inside @media (hover: hover), .ep-btn:hover (5161, 0,2,0, later) sets background --surface-raised but leaves the armed color --ink-inverse, giving white-on-white for an armed phrase under a pointer. All quotes match the file.
