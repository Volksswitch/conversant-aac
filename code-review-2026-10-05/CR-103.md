# CR-103: Transcript live region re-reads the whole conversation, every growing partial word, and every holding phrase

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** low
- **Category:** accessibility
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/index.html:54`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/ui.js:47`; `app/js/ui.js:63`; `app/js/ui.js:145`; `app/js/ui.js:78`; `app/js/app.js:348`; `app/js/app.js:1220`
- **Source:** g-a11y-overflow-01

**What a user notices.** Anyone using a screen reader (Narrator, VoiceOver, TalkBack) hears the entire conversation read out again each time a line is added, and hears the other person's words over and over as they arrive word by word. Because the screen reader talks out loud through the same device, the microphone can pick that speech up as if the other person had said it again.

**What is wrong.** #transcript carries aria-live="polite" with the default aria-relevant (additions text). Every re-render replaces the whole subtree: renderConversation() does transcriptLog.innerHTML='' and re-appends one div per turn, so every existing turn is a fresh addition and the whole history is queued for announcement on every commit. Worse, updatePartnerLive() calls renderConversation() on EVERY speech-recognition interim result once the partner turn has been promoted into history (pendingPartnerHistoryIdx >= 0), so the full history is re-announced several times a second while the partner speaks. When not promoted, setLiveTranscript() changes #liveTurn textContent on every interim result, which is announced as a text change ("Hello", "Hello how", "Hello how are"...). setNowPlaying() and setCoachLine() also live inside the same region, so each placeholder the app speaks through TTS is also read by the screen reader at the same moment (double speech), and tour instructions are read while the tour itself speaks them. The echo filter in stt.js only knows text the app's own TTS spoke (tts notifySpeaking); screen-reader speech is invisible to it, so re-announced partner text and placeholders coming out of the speaker can be transcribed as new partner speech (plausible, not measured).

**How it happens.** Turn on Narrator (Windows) or VoiceOver (iPad). Have a 6-turn conversation. Tap 'Repeat what I said' mid-partner-turn (promotes the partner turn), then let the partner keep talking: the screen reader re-reads all six turns on every word the recognizer delivers. Without the promotion, each partial ('I', 'I went', 'I went to') is read. Each placeholder ('I'm thinking about that.') is spoken twice at once - by the app voice and by the screen reader.

**Suggested fix.** 1) In index.html remove aria-live from #transcript. 2) Put role="log" aria-live="polite" aria-relevant="additions" on #transcriptLog only. 3) Make renderConversation incremental: keep a count of rendered turns; if history grew, append only the new divs; if the length is unchanged and only the last (or the promoted pendingPartnerHistoryIdx) entry's text changed, update that div's textContent in place (set aria-live off for in-place edits by temporarily setting transcriptLog.setAttribute('aria-busy','true') around the update), and only rebuild from scratch when the history shrank (new conversation). 4) Leave #liveTurn, #coachLine and #nowPlaying outside any live region (they are siblings of #transcriptLog, so removing aria-live from #transcript is enough) - the partner's words are announced once, when committed as a turn; placeholders are already audible through TTS.

> **Verifier correction to the fix (takes precedence):** The fix is sound. One addition: if aria-busy is used around in-place edits, it must always be cleared, including on early return or a throw. Otherwise the log goes silent for good. A simpler option is to drop aria-live on #transcript and put role="log" on #transcriptLog only, with renderConversation appending new turns and updating the last turn in place.

**How to verify the fix.** Add tests/transcript-render.test.mjs with a minimal DOM shim (or jsdom-free fake elements) asserting that calling renderConversation with [a,b] then [a,b,c] appends exactly one child and leaves the first two nodes identity-equal. Manually: with Narrator on, have a conversation and confirm each new turn is read once and partial words are not read.

**Do not break.** The transcript must stay fixed-height and scroll to bottom (scrollLogToBottom); keep calling it. Do not move nowPlaying out of #transcript (it must stay visible per UI-Design §7). Paint only - no geometry change, so no keyguard hole moves.
