# CR-138: A sound's 'user acted' bookkeeping happens when it ENDS, inflating response times and leaving cards live during playback

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** low
- **Category:** bug
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/js/app.js:5292`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/app.js:3697-3705 (speakAsUserTurn does this BEFORE speaking)`
- **Source:** app-e-11

**What a user notices.** Playing a sound is recorded as if the person took the whole length of the sound to decide, so the beta's 'how long did they take to answer' figures are wrong for anyone who uses sound buttons, and new suggestion cards can still pop in while the sound plays.

**What is wrong.** playAudioTurn calls noteUserAction('express'), metrics.paletteAbandoned, generationToken++, placeholders.stop() and clearPalette only after `await playClip(item)` returns, i.e. after the clip finishes (clips can be up to ~10 minutes). speakAsUserTurn does the same bookkeeping before it speaks. So decideMs / reading-time metrics include the clip's duration, and an AI request already in flight when the sound was tapped can still land and replace the cards during playback (generationToken was not bumped).

**Evidence.**

```
app/js/app.js:5283 const result = await playClip(item);
app/js/app.js:5292-5300
    noteUserAction('express');
    metrics.paletteAbandoned('express sound');
    metrics.event(metrics.EV.EXPRESS_PHRASE);
    placeholders.stop();
    generationToken++;
    ...
    clearPalette();
app/js/app.js:3697-3705 (speakAsUserTurn) noteUserAction(...); metrics.paletteAbandoned(...); placeholders.stop(); generationToken++;
```

**How it happens.** Cards are up for 3 s; the user taps a 90-second song. The record says the user took 93 s to decide. Meanwhile a reprompt that was in flight renders a fresh set of cards over the playing song.

**Suggested fix.** Move noteUserAction('express'), metrics.paletteAbandoned('express sound'), metrics.event(...), placeholders.stop() and generationToken++ (and ui.setPaletteBusy(false)) to before `await playClip(item)` - they do not await, so they do not break the iPad gesture rule. In the `!result.played` branch, nothing extra is needed (a failed play is still a user action). Keep clearPalette and the commit after the clip.

**How to verify the fix.** Manual with Troubleshooting > recent actions: tap a long sound with cards showing; the recorded decision time matches the tap, not the end of the sound. Source-level guard in tests/express-audio.test.mjs that noteUserAction precedes playClip in playAudioTurn.

**Do not break.** Nothing awaited may be inserted before el.play() (iPad). Do not commit the turn before the clip ends (the abort rule depends on it).

**Design reference.** CLAUDE.md 'EVERY SET OF CARDS IS RECORDED' (outcome and decideMs) and the speakAsUserTurn comment on stopping the clock at the moment the user acts.

**Verification notes.** In playAudioTurn (app.js:5279-5300), noteUserAction('express'), metrics.paletteAbandoned, placeholders.stop() and generationToken++ all run after `await playClip(item)` resolves, which happens when the clip ends or is stopped. noteUserAction computes decideMs as Date.now() - cardsShownAt, so the clip's length is included. Because generationToken is not bumped during playback, an in-flight generation can land and showPalette can replace the cards while the sound plays. The user's eventual outcome is then attributed to the newer set. speakAsUserTurn (3697-3702) does this bookkeeping before it speaks.
