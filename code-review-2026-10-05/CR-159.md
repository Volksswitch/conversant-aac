# CR-159: The echo filter throws away a partner's one-word answer that happens to begin the app's last sentence

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** low
- **Category:** bug
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/js/stt.js:263`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/stt.js:251`; `app/js/stt.js:336`
- **Source:** stt-10

**What a user notices.** If the user just said something like 'Nothing much' and the other person answers 'No.', the app can ignore the answer completely: it is not shown and no new suggestions appear.

**What is wrong.** isEcho()'s prefix rule is character-level (`p.startsWith(t)`), intended for growing INTERIM results of our own playback ('giv', 'give me'). It is applied to FINAL results too, so any final partner segment whose normalized text is a character prefix of an active spoken phrase is dropped for the 4 s match window - which is exactly when the partner replies. 'no' matches 'nothing much...', 'so' matches 'sounds good...', 'i' matches the placeholder 'i m thinking about that'. Because the segment is dropped, no checkpoint fires. CLAUDE.md accepts dropping a partner utterance that 'genuinely repeats our phrase'; a word that is merely the first letters of a different word is not that.

**Evidence.**

```
stt.js:263 `if (p.startsWith(t)) return true;                              // interim prefix`
stt.js:336 `if (isEcho(transcript)) return false;` (called for finals and interims alike)
Experiment (scratchpad exp1.mjs): noteSpokenStart('Nothing much, just relaxing at home.'); noteSpokenEnd(); wait 1.6 s; emitFinal('No.') -> transcript '' and no checkpoint.
```

**How it happens.** User speaks 'Nothing much, just relaxing.' Partner replies 'No?' within 4 s. The final 'no' is treated as echo; nothing appears and no cards are generated for it.

**Suggested fix.** Pass isFinal into isEcho: change ingest() to call `isEcho(transcript, isFinal)` and in isEcho replace the prefix test with `if (isFinal ? (p.startsWith(t + ' ')) : p.startsWith(t)) return true;` so a final only matches at a word boundary (exact match is still covered by `p === t`). Interims keep the sub-word behavior.

**How to verify the fix.** Add to tests/stt.test.mjs: after speaking 'Nothing much, just relaxing.', a final 'No.' within the match window must be kept and fire a checkpoint; an interim 'noth' must still be dropped; a final 'nothing much' must still be dropped. Keep all existing echo tests green.

**Do not break.** Do not weaken the slice/embed/fuzzy rules (CLAUDE.md 'Follow-up 2 — hardened TTS-echo excision'); the accepted cost of dropping a genuine repeat of our whole phrase stays.

**Design reference.** CLAUDE.md 'Transcript mirrors the conversation pane — Follow-up 2' (echo excision)

**Verification notes.** isEcho applies `p.startsWith(t)`, a character-level prefix test, to finals as well as interims (ingest calls isEcho for both). A final 'No.' normalizes to 'no', and 'nothing much just relaxing' starts with 'no', so the segment is dropped as echo during the 4 s match window. No checkpoint fires. The accepted cost in CLAUDE.md covers a partner who genuinely repeats our phrase, not one whose word merely shares the opening letters.
