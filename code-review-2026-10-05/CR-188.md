# CR-188: Removing a person or place leaves their Flex phrase lists behind as 'Someone at somewhere'

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** low
- **Category:** design-violation
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/js/express-panel.js:207`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/worldview-ui.js:746-755`; `app/js/worldview-ui.js:1375-1384`; `app/js/express-editor.js:620-626`; `app/js/express-editor.js:458-470`
- **Source:** express-07

**What a user notices.** When the user removes someone from About Me (or a place from My Places), the phrases they wrote for that person stay stored and show up in the Express Panel settings as 'Someone at Anyplace', with no warning at the time of removal. The person's button on the panel also keeps showing their old name.

**What is wrong.** removeFlexList(key) is documented as 'used when a person or place is deleted', but nothing calls it. The person/place Remove confirmations in worldview-ui.js call rel.removePerson / places.removePlace only. The Express Panel Design states 'deleting a person takes their situations with them, behind a clear warning that says how many'. The orphan lists stay in express-panel.json forever, appear in the editor's 'Already set up' list as 'Someone at somewhere' (situationName fallbacks), and if selected the scope dropdowns show 'Anyone'/'Anyplace' (no option matches the stale id) while the stale list is the one being edited. The Context partner/place buttons that pointed at the removed record also remain, with the editor picker showing '— choose —' beside a button face showing the old name.

**Evidence.**

```
express-panel.js:207-212 `/** Forget one situation — used when a person or place is deleted, and by the editor. */ export function removeFlexList(key) {...}` - grep finds no caller outside express-editor deleteSituation.
worldview-ui.js:748-754 confirm body 'This removes them and your relationship from your profile...' then `await rel.removePerson(p.id);`
express-editor.js:623 `const who = partnerId === ANYONE ? 'Anyone' : (person ? person.name : 'Someone');`
```

**How it happens.** Add Sue in About Me, add a Flex phrase for Sue at Anyplace, then remove Sue in About Me. Settings > Express Panel > Flex 'Already set up' lists 'Someone at Anyplace'; the confirmation when removing Sue never mentioned her phrases.

**Suggested fix.** Add to express-panel.js `export function situationsFor({ partnerId, placeId })` returning the keys whose parseFlexKey matches the id, and `removeSituationsFor(...)` that deletes them in one setModel. In worldview-ui.js person Remove: count `expressPanel.situationsFor({ partnerId: p.id })` phrases and, if any, append to the confirmDanger body e.g. 'Their N Express Panel phrases will be removed too.'; after removePerson, call removeSituationsFor. Same for places with placeId. Also remove Context partner/place buttons whose personId/placeId matches, or leave them but say so in the same warning. Call the Express re-render afterwards.

**How to verify the fix.** Add a test in tests/express-panel-store.test.mjs for situationsFor/removeSituationsFor (keys for 'sue|anyplace' and 'sue|clinic' removed, 'anyone|anyplace' kept). Manually: remove a person with phrases and confirm the warning names the count and the situations disappear from 'Already set up'.

**Do not break.** Deleting must stay behind confirmDanger (standing rule). Also delete any sound files those lists referenced (see the orphaned-audio finding) or they remain in every backup.

**Design reference.** Conversant AAC Express Panel Design.docx 7.3 ('A situation can be deleted... deleting a person takes their situations with them, behind a clear warning that says how many')

**Verification notes.** removeFlexList is called only by the editor's deleteSituation; worldview-ui.js person Remove calls rel.removePerson only, with a confirmation that does not mention phrases, and places.js/relationships.js never touch the Express model. The design document states that deleting a person takes their situations with them behind a warning that says how many. Orphan lists then show as 'Someone at ...' via situationName's fallback.
