# CR-243: The restore confirmation says it replaces saved profiles, but profiles are added beside existing ones and conversations are merged

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** low
- **Category:** ui-inconsistency
- **Verdict:** confirmed (skeptic: confirmed)
- **Location:** `app/js/app.js:6530`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/storage.js:648-680 (importSettingsProfiles renames, never replaces)`; `app/js/data-transfer.js:441-459 (conversations/sounds are added, existing ones kept)`
- **Source:** review-backup-15

**What a user notices.** The warning tells the person their saved profiles will be replaced, when in fact they are kept and the imported ones are added with '(2)' names; and conversations already on the device stay.

**What is wrong.** The confirmDanger body states 'Importing REPLACES what is on this device - your About Me answers, people, Express Panel, starters, settings and saved profiles.' importSettingsProfiles deliberately renames on collision and never deletes existing profiles (Ken, Sept 9 2026), and conversation logs and sounds are written alongside existing ones. The text overstates the destruction and misdescribes what happens to profiles.

**Evidence.**

```
app.js:6530
              `\n\nImporting REPLACES what is on this device — your About Me answers, people, Express Panel, starters, settings and saved profiles. ...`
storage.js:639-644 '⚠ A NAME COLLISION RENAMES THE INCOMING ONE, IT DOES NOT OVERWRITE'
```

**How it happens.** User with profiles 'Home' and 'Clinic' imports a backup with 'Home': warning says profiles will be replaced; afterwards they have 'Home', 'Clinic' and 'Home (2)'.

**Suggested fix.** Reword to: 'Importing REPLACES your About Me answers, people, places, Express Panel, phrases and settings. Saved profiles and conversations in the backup are added to the ones already here. Your keys are left exactly as they are...'.

**How to verify the fix.** Manual read of the dialog; check-docs style American-English rules apply to on-screen text (tests/american-spelling.test.mjs).

**Do not break.** Keep it in confirmDanger (never window.confirm). Update both User Manuals if they quote this wording.

**Design reference.** CLAUDE.md 'Confirm before destroying significant work'

**Verification notes.** The confirmDanger body (app.js ~6530) says importing 'REPLACES ... settings and saved profiles'. importSettingsProfiles (storage.js ~648) never deletes or overwrites a profile: a colliding name gets ' (n)' (Ken, September 9 2026). Conversation logs and sounds are written alongside existing ones. The wording overstates what is replaced and misdescribes profiles; the restart card even says 'nothing of yours was replaced' for renamed profiles, which contradicts the confirmation.
