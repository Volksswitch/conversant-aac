# CR-270: "Ask them to repeat" pressed with no conversation records a turn and opens a repair

Part of the [October 5, 2026 code review](README.md). Read its instructions before fixing.

- **Severity:** low
- **Category:** bug
- **Verdict:** plausible (skeptic: plausible)
- **Location:** `app/js/app.js:3280`
- **Also affects:** `app/js/app.js:3286`; `app/js/app.js:3299`
- **Source:** live-runtime-09

**What a user notices.** A stray tap on "Ask them to repeat" before anyone has spoken says the phrase, writes it into the conversation record as if a conversation had started, and makes the app treat the other person's first words as a repeat of something they never said.

**What is wrong.** handlePardon has no guard for 'nothing to repeat': it calls engine.pardon() (pushing a REPAIR sequence onto an empty stack), speaks, and logSpokenUserTurn commits the phrase, which lazily creates a conversation log. handleSayAgain, by contrast, returns with 'Nothing to repeat yet' when there is nothing.

**Evidence.**

```
app/js/app.js:3286  const snap = engine.pardon(); // push REPAIR* (dedups); floor → partner
app/js/app.js:3298-3299
    await speakUserStatement(text);
    logSpokenUserTurn(text);
Runtime: right after Start, tapping Ask them to repeat then End left the line "Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you sa..." in the transcript.
```

**How it happens.** Start, then tap 'Ask them to repeat' before Listen or any partner speech: the pardon appears in the transcript and a conversation file is created.

> **Verifier correction to the trigger:** 'Before anyone has spoken' cannot be known by the app. The realistic trigger is a stray tap, or a deliberate tap when the partner spoke before Listen was on. In the second case the current behavior is arguably correct.

**Suggested fix.** Ask Ken whether a pardon at rest should still be spoken (it may be useful in person). If the record is the only problem: at the top of handlePardon, compute `const kept = heardPartnerText();` first and, when `!kept && conversationHistory.length === 0`, speak the phrase with `speakUserStatement(text, { announce: true })` but skip engine.pardon() and logSpokenUserTurn.

**How to verify the fix.** Manual: Start, tap Ask them to repeat - phrase is spoken (and shown in the now-playing line) but no transcript line and no conversations/ file; with a partner turn present, behavior unchanged.

**Do not break.** Do not change the July 13 2026 behavior when a partner turn exists (kept as its own turn, re-speak starts fresh).

**Design reference.** CLAUDE.md 'Follow-up 4 - Ask them to repeat re-speak = a separate turn'

**Verification notes.** The mechanism is as described: handlePardon has no 'nothing heard' guard (unlike handleSayAgain), and logSpokenUserTurn commits the phrase, which lazily starts a conversation log. The framing as a defect is doubtful, though. A pardon with nothing captured has a legitimate in-person use: the partner spoke while the mic was off, so the app cannot know whether anyone spoke. The phrase is said aloud in the user's voice, and the record standard ('recreate the conversation to the second') favors writing it down. Pushing a repair so the partner's restatement resolves against it is also reasonable there. No recorded decision either way, so it needs Ken's call rather than a fix.
