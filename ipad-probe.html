Conversant AAC — iPad Capability Probe

Establishes, on real hardware, which browser capabilities Conversant AAC depends on actually work. Run it in Safari, then Chrome, then Edge — and in each, both as a normal tab and as a Home Screen app (Share → Add to Home Screen). Send the report at the bottom back after each run.

Order matters. Run the sections top to bottom. The passive checks fill in automatically; the rest need a tap, because iOS only grants microphone, speech, and audio from a real user gesture.

1 · Environment

2 · Viewport & layout

Conversant's layout is calibrated to ~1280–1465 × 853–976 CSS px and sets height: 100vh with overflow: hidden. If the visible height is smaller than 100vh, the bottom of the app (the Express Panel / keyboard dock) falls under the browser toolbar and cannot be tapped.

3 · Speech OUT (text-to-speech)

Conversant speaks placeholder phrases on a timer, not on a tap — so whether speak() works without a fresh gesture is decisive. Practice Mode additionally needs two distinct voices.

4 · Speech IN (recognition) — the decisive test

Conversant sets continuous = true and interimResults = true, and fires generation after a silence period. Both flags are reported broken or unreliable on iOS. Each test listens for 12 seconds. Say a couple of sentences, then stop talking and wait — the pause is part of the test.

5 · Microphone capture (the cloud-STT fallback path)

If the built-in recognition fails, the fallback streams microphone audio to a paid transcription service. That needs getUserMedia, an encoder, and AudioWorklet.

6 · Storage

The app's data folder becomes OPFS on iPad. Durability depends on navigator.storage.persist(); without it, WebKit may evict everything after 7 days of no use.

7 · On-screen keyboard suppression

Conversant draws its own keyboard on the keyguard grid and must stop the OS keyboard from appearing. It uses inputmode="none", which is documented as unreliable on iOS; readonly is the usual workaround. Tap each box. Detection watches for a visualViewport height change.
