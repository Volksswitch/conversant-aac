Conversant switch access prototype: In my own words

Anywhere on this page except the panel on the right, the left mouse button is switch 1 and the right mouse button is switch 2. Space and Enter do the same. With three switches, the middle mouse button is switch 3, which moves back; keys 1, 2 and 3 also work. The screen copies the app's “In my own words” window: the text box, Speak, Reframe and Cancel, and the app's keyboard. Try the tasks on the right, then change how the scan works and try them again.     How the scan works

    Number of switches

    Choosing One, Two or Three puts the other scan settings back to that choice’s starting values. Keys can be set below.

    Switch keys
    Switch 1:

    For a switch interface that sends key presses. Tap “Set”, then press the key (or the switch). Setting a key turns off the mouse buttons as switches; check the box to turn them back on. The mouse still works in this panel. These choices are remembered on this device.

    Scan method

    One switch: automatic, inverse or hold to move. Two or three switches: step. In step scanning, holding a button that moves keeps the highlight moving (racing).

    Seconds per stop
    Top speed while a switch is held (seconds per stop)
    Speeding up while held

    While a switch is held, the highlight starts at “Seconds per stop”, and each stop then comes a little sooner until it reaches the top speed. Set a top speed slower than “Seconds per stop” and holding slows it down instead.

    Tap or hold

    Step: holding a button that moves keeps the highlight moving once the press is a hold. Hold to move: the first stop comes when the press becomes a hold, then the scan speeds up as set above, so a short hold moves one stop.

    How the scan moves

    Either way the scan starts inside the keyboard and goes round it until “Leave keyboard”, always its last stop, which goes to Speak, Reframe and Cancel and then the Command Bar. A row of keys, Speak/Reframe/Cancel and the Command Bar are each left after a pass with no press. One line suits racing with two or three switches.

    After a key

    Then the scan starts again at the top of the keyboard. The next letter is further along the same row only about 1 time in 8.

    Keyboard

    The third has QWERTY's shape and keys, with the letters ordered by how often they're used.

    Word suggestion

    The suggestion is also the gray ending in the text box. As a key, it sits in Enter’s place (a line break means nothing in spoken words), so no key is lost and nothing moves; it is blank, and skipped, when there is no suggestion. As a stop, the text box itself is highlighted. Either way, choosing it finishes the word, adds a space, and starts the scan again at the top of the keyboard.

    Stray and mistimed presses
    One press or two

    Keys never need two presses, as in the app. “End” and “Don’t save” always ask for a second press.

    Sound

    Settings take effect at once and restart the scan. “Pause”, at the end of the Command Bar, stops the scan; hold a switch for 2 seconds to start it again. “Settings” on the Command Bar is skipped. In the app, “Hold on” and “Don’t save” leave the window open; every other Command Bar button closes it first, as Cancel does.
