Tasks

    A task is checked when the setting reaches its target. The cost is counted from the previous task you finished.

    How the scan works

    The scan starts on the tabs and Close. Choosing a tab puts its sections in the tab’s place, so the scan goes round the other tabs, the chosen tab’s sections and Close in one cycle. Pressing a section heading opens that section, and its settings take the heading’s place in the same cycle.

    Number of switches

    Choosing One, Two or Three puts the other scan settings back to that choice’s starting values. Keys can be set below.

    Scan method

    One switch: automatic, inverse or hold to move. Two or three switches: step. In step scanning, holding a button that moves keeps the highlight moving (racing).

    Seconds per stop
    Top speed while a switch is held (seconds per stop)
    Speeding up while held

    While a switch is held, the highlight starts at “Seconds per stop”, and each stop then comes a little sooner until it reaches the top speed. Set a top speed slower than “Seconds per stop” and holding slows it down instead.

    Tap or hold

    Step: holding a button that moves keeps the highlight moving once the press is a hold. Hold to move: the first stop comes when the press becomes a hold, then the scan speeds up as set above, so a short hold moves one stop.

    How the scan moves

    Not used in Settings, so it can’t be changed here. Choosing a tab or opening a section is moving around the panel, not grouping, and the settings inside a section are already one list.

    Drop-down lists
    Leaving a group

    Applies to an open section, the chosen tab, a row in a list (such as a phrase with its boxes and buttons), a drop-down list and the keyboard.
