The microphone stays on for the whole exchange. Nothing is spoken by the app while they are still talking.
They pauseQuiet for the Silence Period — half a second by default
Two things start here, and they are independent: the app asks the AI for suggestions, and the holding-phrase clock starts.
A holding phrase is spokenTwo seconds after the pause — "I'm thinking about that."
It fills the silence while you read. It is not an answer, and it never waits for the AI.
Four suggestions appearA plain reply, a way to decline, a way to take the lead, and a way to ask
Usually four to eight seconds after the pause. Until then the panel keeps whatever was there before — on the first exchange, nothing.
If the other person starts talking again, the holding phrases stop at once and their words keep being added. At their next pause the suggestions are replaced by a fresh set — the panel is never emptied while you are reading it.
