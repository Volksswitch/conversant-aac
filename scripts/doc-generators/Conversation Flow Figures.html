Four suggestions are on screen, and the other person is waitingNothing happens until you act. Every one of the following is available at that moment.

Tap a suggestionOne of the four the AI wrote. The commonest way to answer.
Tap an Express Panel phraseYes, no, thank you. Said at once, without waiting for anything.
Type it, then tap SpeakYour own words, said in your own voice.
The suggestions clear, what was said is recorded, and listening either resumes on its own or waits for you to tap Listen.
You say something, but you are not answering
Hold onSays a holding phrase you picked, instead of waiting for the one the clock would pick. The suggestions stay in front of you.
Repeat what I saidSays your last words again, for when they did not catch them. The suggestions stay in front of you.
Ask them to repeatAsks them to say it again. What was already heard is kept, and their repeat starts a fresh turn rather than being run onto the end of the old one. This one does clear the suggestions, because you have handed the floor back.
Two of these leave you exactly where you were. The third hands the floor back and waits for them.

The first request is not shown — it is thrown away.Its answer comes back at 10.3s, by which time the app has already asked again with the fuller sentence, so the older answer is discarded rather than put on screen. That is why someone who keeps going pushes the wait out: for practical purposes the clock restarts at their last pause.

Ending a conversation is two steps, and it can be started from either side. The branch on the right is the one people do not expect.

You decide to finish
They start closing first
You press Wrap upStatements that signal you are finishing, without saying goodbye yet. Press it again to put back what it covered.
You choose one, and it is spoken“I should get going.” “It's been good talking to you.”
They start closing“Anyway…” “I should let you go.” The app notices, and offers the goodbyes without being asked.
The goodbyes are offeredChoosing one speaks it, and then offers them again, in case they say goodbye back.
“Actually, before you go —”Offered only when they started the closing, because that is the one moment at which declining it is the natural thing to say.
Back to the conversationThe closing is abandoned, and the loop in Figure 1 carries on.
End conversationStops listening, saves what was said, and clears the screen. The next Start begins a fresh record.
