The moment the flow chart calls "What do you do next?", opened out. Grouped by what each one does to your turn, not by where the button is.

Four suggestions are on screen, and the other person is waitingNothing happens until you act. Every one of the following is available at that moment.
You answer — and the exchange is done
Tap a suggestionOne of the four the AI wrote. The commonest way to answer.
Tap an Express Panel phraseYes, no, thank you. Said at once, without waiting for anything.
Type it, then tap SpeakYour own words, said in your own voice.
The suggestions clear, what was said is recorded, and listening either resumes on its own or waits for you to tap Listen.
You say something, but you are not answering
Hold onSays a holding phrase you picked, instead of waiting for the one the clock would pick. The suggestions stay in front of you.
Repeat what I saidSays your last words again, for when they did not catch them. The suggestions stay in front of you.
Ask them to repeatAsks them to say it again. What was already heard is kept, and their repeat starts a fresh turn rather than being run onto the end of the old one. This one does clear the suggestions, because you have handed the floor back.
Two of these leave you exactly where you were. The third hands the floor back and waits for them.
