Always band the words that stay in the same place forever. Never changes.
Filtered band phrases filtered by the partner and place selected below. The only band whose contents change.
Context band the buttons that never speak. They tell the app who the partner is, where the user is, how they feel.

The dividing line that makes this learnable: a button in the Context band never says anything out loud. Everywhere else, a button speaks. Feeling does not filter the middle band; only partner and place do.

Roles — what stays the same when the words change

Each position in the Filtered band stands for a kind of thing to say. The words change with the partner; the kind does not. Greetings and goodbyes are deliberately absent — those already vary by partner in the response palette, so repeating them here would be two places to maintain.
